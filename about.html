About

A multidisciplinary view of wellbeing.

Dr. Balasingham Arasabalan is a Toronto-based professional working at the intersection of clinical psychology, mental health, social work, psychological assessment and clinical research.

With a PhD in Clinical Psychology and professional experience in Ontario's mental-health and social-work environments, his work reflects a multidisciplinary approach to understanding psychological wellbeing and its relationship with behavioural, social and physiological factors.
